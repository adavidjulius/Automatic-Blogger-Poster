Correction Policy

ReadContext is committed to correcting errors promptly and transparently. If you believe a fact, statistic, or attribution in one of our published articles is inaccurate, please email us with the article URL, the specific claim in question, and credible documentation supporting the correction. Validated corrections are updated immediately with an explicit editorial correction note.

Response Time

Our desk typically reviews and responds to general inquiries within 24 to 48 business hours. Urgent editorial corrections receive priority review.

Welcome to ReadContext (readcontext.blogspot.com). At ReadContext, we respect the privacy of our visitors and are committed to protecting the personal information you may share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to safeguard your data in compliance with international privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Information We Collect

Like most digital publications and blogs, ReadContext collects information to improve reader experience, monitor site performance, and serve relevant content.

  • Log Files: We collect routine technical data through standard server logs, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, operating system, date/time stamp, and clickstream data. This information is non-personally identifiable and is analyzed solely to administer the site and understand user navigation patterns.
  • Voluntary Correspondence: If you contact us directly via email or our contact forms, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message to respond to your inquiry.
  • Comments and Submissions: If you leave comments on articles, your public display name, comment text, and submitted website URL may be visible to other readers.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

ReadContext uses cookies (small text files stored on your device) to store preferences, optimize web pages based on browser type, and personalize content. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, specifically DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to readcontext.blogspot.com and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at Google Ads Privacy Policy.

Our advertising partners and analytics providers may use cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on ReadContext. These technologies automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Please note that ReadContext has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

4. GDPR Data Protection Rights (European Users)

If you reside within the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific statutory data rights, including:

  • The right to access personal data held about you.
  • The right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten").

  • The right to data portability.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (California Consumers)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion of collected data, and opt-out of the sale of personal information. ReadContext does not sell personal consumer information.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Protecting children's privacy is especially important. ReadContext does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that ReadContext has in its database personal data of a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.
